Event Description

Howdy Hackers!

Join us for a weekend of innovation and creativity as we build projects that enhance your personal portfolios or assist individuals in improving their own profiles. Whether you're a seasoned professional or just starting out, this is the perfect opportunity to showcase your skills and make a meaningful impact.

Whether you build something on the theme or not, focus on having fun. While we'd love to have you build something on the theme, our themes are only here for inspiration. Please build whatever you'd like!
